## Intent

Close the twenty-seven M2 Babylon.js finite dependent-map exports with exact locked discriminator correlations

## Evidence obligations

- `EV001` (`verification`): Generate the 8 DeviceType, 6 FlowGraph asset, and 22 WebXR feature rows twice and prove complete, unique, non-collapsed byte-identical tables.
- `EV002` (`test`): Compile one positive fixture per discriminator and reject negative option, input, result, and specialization mismatches.
- `EV003` (`test`): Fable-compile representative calls, inspect exact emitted imports, and run meaningful Node module/runtime smoke.
- `EV004` (`test`): Run deterministic Chromium XR smoke with WebXR browser stubs and no physical device or session requirement.
- `EV005` (`verification`): Regenerate coverage and prove exactly 4,279 typed, 43 blocked, one runtime-only, zero lossy, with exactly the named twenty-seven exports reclassified.
- `EV006` (`test`): Pack and consume the maintained binding from a clean external project with exact npm dependencies, imports, runtime, and Chromium proof.

## Extension

## User Value

Fable consumers can use Babylon.js Device input, FlowGraph, and XR/VR APIs while compile-time input, options, and result types stay exactly correlated to their finite discriminator.

## Scope

- SB-001: Close exactly the seven Device input, nine FlowGraph, and eleven XR/VR exports named by roadmap M2 over Babylon.js 9.19.0.
- SB-002: Preserve exact finite singleton, overload, and specialized views for 8 DeviceType, 6 FlowGraph asset, and 22 WebXR feature rows, plus exact imports and type-only boundaries.
